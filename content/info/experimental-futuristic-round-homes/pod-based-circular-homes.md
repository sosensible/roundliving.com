# Pod-Based Circular Homes

Pod-based circular homes organize multiple modular round units around shared radial connections. This allows phased construction, functional separation, and long-term adaptability as household needs change.

## Defining Features

- Modular circular pods connected by radial links or shared hubs
- Flexible program distribution across multiple units
- Expandable systems for phased growth and budget staging

## Typical Configurations

- One central social pod with surrounding private pods
- Paired living/sleeping pods with service pods between
- Clustered multi-household compounds with shared commons

## Advantages

- Incremental construction without full upfront build-out
- Easier replacement or upgrade of individual modules
- Clear separation of noisy and quiet functions

## Considerations

- Connector weatherproofing and thermal continuity need careful detailing
- Site planning must account for circulation between pods
- Mechanical and utility distribution can become complex at larger scales
