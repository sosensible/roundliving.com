# Spherical Homes

Spherical homes extend circular architecture into three dimensions, using full or partial sphere envelopes. This geometry offers high enclosure efficiency and a distinctive spatial experience that differs from both standard domes and conventional houses.

## Defining Features

- Full-sphere or major-partial-sphere building form
- Continuous curved envelope with efficient surface-to-volume ratio
- Highly immersive interior spatial character

## Why Designers Explore Spheres

- Potential energy advantages from reduced envelope area
- Strong structural behavior when loads follow curved shells
- Iconic architectural identity for experimental residential design

## Considerations

- Floor insertion and usable headroom planning can be complex
- Openings, waterproofing, and detailing require high precision
- Interior fit-out often needs custom furniture and joinery
