# Experimental & Futuristic Round Homes

Experimental circular housing pushes beyond conventional methods toward adaptive, rapid, and technology-driven systems. These typologies often prioritize speed, modularity, environmental response, and novel spatial experiences.

## Quick Comparison

- **Inflatable domes** are lightweight and rapid to deploy for temporary use.
- **3D-printed homes** reduce material waste through additive fabrication.
- **Rotating homes** optimize solar orientation and view control dynamically.
- **Spherical homes** maximize enclosure efficiency with full or near-full sphere geometry.
- **Pod-based systems** support modular growth through radial clusters.

Explore each type to compare feasibility, construction method, and long-term livability.
