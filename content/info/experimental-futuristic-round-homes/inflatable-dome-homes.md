# Inflatable Dome Homes

Inflatable dome homes use internal air pressure or inflatable structural components to create enclosed circular volume quickly. They are especially useful when speed, portability, and low transport weight are key requirements.

## Defining Features

- Air-supported or inflatable membrane structures
- Fast setup and takedown with compact transport profiles
- Lightweight materials with high enclosure efficiency
- Often used for temporary, seasonal, or emergency deployment

## Common Use Cases

- Disaster-relief or humanitarian shelter programs
- Temporary workforce and field-research housing
- Event-based and mobile hospitality structures

## Considerations

- Continuous or managed pressure systems may be required
- Durability depends on membrane material quality and maintenance
- Thermal and acoustic performance varies with insulation strategy
- Long-term permanence may be limited by local code frameworks
