# Rotating Circular Homes

Rotating circular homes are designed to turn around a central axis, often up to 360 degrees, so occupants can optimize orientation to sunlight, weather, or views throughout the day and seasons.

## Defining Features

- Circular superstructure on a rotating platform
- Orientation control for daylight, passive solar gain, and shading
- Dynamic relationship between interior spaces and landscape views

## Potential Advantages

- Improved winter solar capture and summer heat management
- Flexible view framing without relocating rooms
- Distinctive experiential quality and high architectural novelty

## Key Technical Considerations

- Rotation mechanics and maintenance access are critical
- Utility routing must support controlled movement
- Wind load behavior changes with orientation and must be engineered
- Backup/manual mode planning is important for resilience
