# 3D-Printed Round Homes

3D-printed round homes use additive construction techniques to form curved wall systems directly from digital models. Circular and dome-adjacent geometries are particularly compatible with this approach because the printer can trace smooth non-orthogonal paths efficiently.

## Defining Features

- Additive layer-by-layer wall fabrication
- Circular, curvilinear, or dome-like shell geometry
- Reduced material waste compared with subtractive methods
- High repeatability from digital-to-physical workflows

## Why This Typology Is Growing

- Fast shell construction timelines
- Lower labor intensity for repetitive wall forms
- Strong fit for custom curved geometry without extensive formwork

## Considerations

- Mechanical, electrical, and plumbing integration must be planned early
- Regional code acceptance is still evolving
- Material performance and long-term durability depend on mix design and QA
