# Circular Courtyard Homes

Circular courtyard homes pair an enclosing round perimeter with a central open-air courtyard. This arrangement creates a protected inward-facing microclimate while preserving curved circulation and spatial continuity.

## Defining Features

- Round outer wall or ring-like built edge
- Central courtyard used for light, air, and social space
- Continuous circulation around an inner open core
- Strong privacy from exterior-facing openings

## Performance Benefits

- Improved passive ventilation through stack and cross-flow effects
- Daylight brought deep into plan via internal courtyard edge
- Better acoustic and visual buffering from surroundings

## Design Considerations

- Courtyard proportion is critical for sun and shade balance
- Drainage and waterproofing details at courtyard interfaces must be robust
- Privacy strategy should align with local climate and neighborhood context
