# Curvilinear Concrete Homes

Curvilinear concrete homes apply freeform curved geometry using shotcrete, cast-in-place formwork, or hybrid systems. They are often dome-adjacent in spirit but not fully spherical, allowing more tailored spatial compositions.

## Defining Features

- Continuous or segmented freeform concrete shells
- Smooth curve transitions between wall, roof, and opening surfaces
- Strong mass and durability with expressive plastic form
- Flexible geometry for custom site and program response

## Typical Construction Approaches

- Shotcrete over rebar cages for complex curves
- CNC-cut formwork for precision cast surfaces
- Hybrid systems combining concrete shells with steel or timber supports

## Design Considerations

- Formwork and craftsmanship quality strongly affect outcome
- Waterproofing, drainage, and crack control are critical details
- Thermal strategy must balance mass with insulation placement
