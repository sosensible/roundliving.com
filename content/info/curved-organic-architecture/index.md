# Curved & Organic Architecture

Curved and organic round-home design moves beyond strict circles into oval, shell-like, and freeform geometries. These typologies prioritize spatial flow, sculptural expression, and close integration with landscape and light.

- [Curvilinear Concrete Homes](/info/curved-organic-architecture/curvilinear-concrete-homes)
- [Shell Structures](/info/curved-organic-architecture/shell-structures)
- [Circular Courtyard Homes](/info/curved-organic-architecture/circular-courtyard-homes)

## Quick Comparison

- **Elliptical homes** use oval footprints to shape circulation and sightlines.
- **Curvilinear concrete homes** use shotcrete or custom formwork for fluid geometry.
- **Shell structures** derive form from natural load-efficient curves.
- **Circular courtyard homes** combine protected outer enclosure with a central open-air void.
