# Elliptical Homes

Elliptical homes use an oval plan instead of a pure circle, creating subtle directional emphasis while preserving curved spatial continuity. This geometry is common in modern sculptural architecture where movement, framing, and form are equally important.

## Defining Features

- Oval footprint with major and minor axes
- Curved perimeter walls with directional interior flow
- Strong control of approach, axis, and view framing
- Distinctive sculptural external expression

## Why Designers Use Ellipses

- Better response to elongated sites than full circles
- Natural zoning along long and short axes
- Opportunities for dramatic compression and expansion in sequence

## Considerations

- Custom glazing and envelope detailing can raise costs
- Structural coordination is more complex than orthogonal plans
- Interior fit-out often requires bespoke joinery
