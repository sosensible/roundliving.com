# Earth-Sheltered Domes

Earth-sheltered domes combine the structural efficiency of dome geometry with the thermal and protective benefits of surrounding soil. Depending on site and design goals, these homes may be partially bermed, three-quarter buried, or integrated into a hillside.

## Defining Features

- Curved shell geometry designed to carry loads efficiently
- Earth berming or burial over portions of the structure
- Reduced exterior envelope exposure to wind and temperature swings
- Stable interior conditions with lower heating and cooling demand

## Performance Benefits

- Excellent energy efficiency from thermal buffering
- Strong weather resilience in high-wind and storm-prone locations
- Reduced visual impact through landscape integration
- Potential acoustic damping from earth cover

## Design Considerations

- Waterproofing and drainage systems are non-negotiable
- Site hydrology and soil conditions must guide foundation strategy
- Daylighting requires intentional placement of openings and courtyards
- Ventilation and humidity control need careful mechanical planning

## Typical Use Cases

- Off-grid or low-energy homesteads
- Climate-responsive homes in hot or cold regions
- Sites with slopes suitable for cut-and-berm approaches
