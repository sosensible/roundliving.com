# Earth Integrated

Earth-integrated round homes combine circular geometry with earthen materials or site integration strategies. These approaches often emphasize thermal stability, low embodied energy, and close landscape connection.

## Building Types

- [Earthbag Roundhouses](/info/earth-integrated/earthbag-roundhouses)
- [Earth-Sheltered Domes](/info/earth-integrated/earth-sheltered-domes)
- [Hobbit-Style Round Homes](/info/earth-integrated/hobbit-style-round-homes)

## Quick Comparison

- **Earthbag roundhouses** prioritize low-cost construction and robust wall systems using local fill.
- **Earth-sheltered domes** use surrounding earth mass for strong energy performance.
- **Hobbit-style homes** blend into hillsides with organic forms and landscape-led design.
