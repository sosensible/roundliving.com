# Beaver Lodges

Beaver lodges are dome-like shelters built from branches, saplings, mud, and plant material. Their rounded mass helps them resist weather, hold heat, and protect the chamber inside from predators.

## Defining Features

- Layered shell of sticks sealed with mud
- Central living chamber kept above the waterline
- Underwater entrances that add security and thermal buffering
- Continuous repair and expansion over time

## Architectural Lessons

- Layered envelopes can combine structure, insulation, and weatherproofing
- Compact rounded forms reduce exposed surface area
- Protected entry paths can improve security and environmental control

## Why They Matter Here

Beaver lodges show that efficient round shelter is not just a human idea. They are practical examples of enclosure, maintenance, and climate adaptation using only locally available material.
