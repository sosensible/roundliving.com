# Lighthouse Homes

Lighthouse homes adapt the iconic lighthouse silhouette into full-time living spaces. Their cylindrical geometry and vertical organization make them distinctive options for narrow or scenic sites.

## Defining Features

- Cylindrical, frequently tapering massing
- Multi-level floor stacking with compact footprints
- Panoramic upper-level living rooms, studios, or lookout spaces
- Stair-led circulation as a central architectural element

## Typical Layout Pattern

- **Ground level**: entry, utility, storage, and service spaces
- **Middle levels**: bedrooms, bathrooms, and day-to-day living support
- **Top level**: primary shared living area oriented to long views

This inverted arrangement places the most view-sensitive spaces at the highest point.

## Design Advantages

- Exceptional access to views and daylight
- Strong identity and landmark quality
- Efficient use of constrained parcels
- Natural wind-shedding geometry in exposed coastal settings

## Considerations

- Vertical circulation can be strenuous for some occupants
- Stair and egress code requirements are critical early constraints
- Curved walls complicate cabinetry and furniture planning
- Wind and moisture detailing are especially important near coasts
