# Observation Tower Homes

Observation tower homes are contemporary, view-first dwellings that elevate key living spaces above surrounding terrain. They are often designed for forests, coastal edges, wetlands, or mountain sites where layered views and environmental immersion are central to the project brief.

## Defining Features

- Tall, slender cylindrical or faceted forms
- Modern steel or concrete structural systems
- Strategic glazing oriented to specific view corridors
- Top-floor social spaces or observation rooms

## Common Site Contexts

- Forest clearings with canopy-level views
- Coastal ridges and bluff edges
- Lakeside or marsh landscapes
- Remote retreat properties with broad horizons

## Structural Approaches

- **Steel frame + composite floors** for speed and precision
- **Reinforced concrete cores** for stiffness and durability
- **Hybrid systems** balancing thermal mass, embodied carbon, and cost

## Design Considerations

- Lateral wind loads become a primary design driver
- Foundation design must match geotechnical conditions on exposed sites
- Envelope performance is critical due to high wind and weather exposure
- Vertical circulation, emergency egress, and maintenance access should be solved early
