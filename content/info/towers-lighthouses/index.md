# Towers & Lighthouses

Towers and lighthouse-inspired homes emphasize vertical living, dramatic views, and small-footprint site use. They are ideal when land is constrained or when elevation and panoramic sightlines are a design priority.

## Building Types

- [Lighthouse Homes](/info/towers-lighthouses/lighthouse-homes)
- [Tower Houses](/info/towers-lighthouses/tower-houses)
- [Observation Tower Homes](/info/towers-lighthouses/observation-tower-homes)

## Quick Comparison

- **Lighthouse homes** typically use tapering cylindrical forms and place social spaces near the upper levels.
- **Tower houses** are often medieval-inspired, with compact stacked floor plates for dense sites.
- **Observation tower homes** are usually modern steel or concrete structures designed around views and landscape immersion.
