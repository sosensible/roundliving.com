# Traditional Mongolian Yurts (Gers)

The Mongolian *ger* is one of the most refined portable dwellings ever developed. It combines a collapsible timber lattice with layered felt or canvas covers, producing a circular home that is both warm and rapidly deployable.

## Defining Features

- Wood lattice wall sections that fold for transport
- Roof poles radiating to a central crown ring
- Felt or canvas cover layers for insulation and weather protection
- Circular planning with a highly efficient structural logic

## Why Gers Endure

- Fast assembly and disassembly for mobile lifestyles
- Excellent thermal comfort when properly layered
- Long cultural lineage of practical climate adaptation

## Considerations

- Cover maintenance and periodic replacement are expected
- Ventilation strategy is important to reduce condensation
- Local permitting may classify gers as temporary or permanent depending on setup
