# Yurts

Yurts combine circular living with practical shelter design, ranging from traditional portable systems to code-compliant and hard-shell permanent installations.

## Yurt Building Types

- [Traditional Mongolian Yurts (Gers)](/info/yurts/traditional-mongolian-yurts-gers)
- [Modern Engineered Yurts](/info/yurts/modern-engineered-yurts)
- [Hard-Shell Yurts](/info/yurts/hard-shell-yurts)

## Quick Comparison

- **Traditional Mongolian yurts (gers)** use wood lattice walls with felt or canvas, offering portable warmth and circular efficiency.
- **Modern engineered yurts** are insulated, code-aware systems often used as full-time homes.
- **Hard-shell yurts** combine circular footprints with rigid wall systems for cold climates and permanent installations.

Choose based on your priority: portability, full-time performance, or permanent cold-climate durability.
