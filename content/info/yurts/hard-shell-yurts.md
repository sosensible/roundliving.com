# Hard-Shell Yurts

Hard-shell yurts combine the circular planning advantages of traditional yurts with rigid wall and roof assemblies. These systems are typically chosen when long-term permanence, colder climate performance, or reduced maintenance is the priority.

## Defining Features

- Circular footprint with rigid structural wall systems
- Higher envelope durability than fabric-only systems
- Better suitability for heavy snow, wind, and freeze-thaw conditions
- Often configured as permanent installations

## Why People Choose Hard-Shell Systems

- Improved long-term weather resilience
- More conventional interior finishes and cabinetry options
- Reduced maintenance cycles compared with fabric structures

## Considerations

- Upfront cost is generally higher than soft-shell yurts
- Transport and assembly can require heavier equipment
- Detail quality at panel joints and roof transitions is critical
