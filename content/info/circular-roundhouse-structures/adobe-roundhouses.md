# Adobe Roundhouses

Adobe roundhouses use sun-dried earthen blocks laid in circular courses to create thick, high-mass walls. This approach is especially effective in climates with strong day-night temperature swings.

## Defining Features

- Curved masonry walls made from adobe units
- High thermal mass and interior temperature moderation
- Material systems based on clay-rich soils and natural aggregates
- Strong longevity when protected from persistent moisture

## Performance Notes

- Thick walls absorb daytime heat and release it later, smoothing indoor temperature cycles
- Openings and lintel design require careful structural coordination in curved walls

## Considerations

- Weather protection and foundation drainage are crucial
- Seismic reinforcement strategies may be required in active regions
- Quality control in block production affects long-term durability
