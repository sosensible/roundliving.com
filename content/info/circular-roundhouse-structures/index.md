# Circular & Roundhouse Structures

Roundhouse structures span deep building traditions and contemporary engineered approaches. This section compares key roundhouse families by material system, construction logic, and performance profile.

## Quick Comparison

- **Traditional roundhouses** are climate-adapted vernacular forms found across many cultures.
- **Modern roundhouses** use engineered steel or timber systems for precision and performance.
- **Cob roundhouses** emphasize hand-sculpted earthen walls and low-embodied-energy building.
- **Adobe roundhouses** use thick earthen blocks for thermal stability.
- **Stone roundhouses** combine long-life masonry with revived ecological design intent.
