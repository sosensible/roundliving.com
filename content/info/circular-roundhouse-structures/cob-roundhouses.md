# Cob Roundhouses

Cob roundhouses are built from a hand-mixed earthen material typically composed of clay, sand, straw, and water. The method supports thick, sculptural curved walls and a highly tactile architectural language.

## Defining Features

- Monolithic earthen wall construction
- Organic curved geometry and integrated built-in forms
- Low embodied energy when local soils are used
- Strong affinity with owner-build and natural building communities

## Advantages

- High thermal mass and humidity-buffering behavior
- Flexible hand-shaped design possibilities
- Minimal industrial processing compared with many modern systems

## Considerations

- Protecting walls from bulk water is critical
- Roof overhangs, capillary breaks, and foundation detailing are essential
- Building-code acceptance may vary by location
