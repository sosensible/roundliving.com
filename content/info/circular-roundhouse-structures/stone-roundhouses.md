# Stone Roundhouses

Stone roundhouses combine ancient masonry logic with contemporary ecological design goals. Their heavy circular walls offer long service life, strong weather resilience, and a deeply rooted material expression.

## Defining Features

- Circular stone wall systems with conical, domed, or timber-supported roofs
- Very high durability and resistance to weathering
- Significant thermal mass for passive performance support
- Strong regional identity through local stone use

## Why They Are Returning

- Interest in long-life, low-maintenance building envelopes
- Appreciation for craft-based, place-specific architecture
- Desire for robust homes with low replacement cycles

## Considerations

- Skilled stonemasonry is labor intensive and can drive cost
- Structural engineering is important for openings and roof interface details
- Embodied energy depends on quarry distance and construction method
