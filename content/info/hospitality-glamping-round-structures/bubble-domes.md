# Bubble Domes

Bubble domes are lightweight spherical guest units made from transparent or semi-transparent membranes. They are widely used in glamping and destination hospitality to create a high-immersion experience with panoramic views.

## Defining Features

- Clear or translucent spherical enclosure
- Strong visual connection to sky, landscape, and weather
- Compact layouts for short-stay hospitality use
- Distinctive novelty appeal for premium bookings

## Operational Benefits

- Memorable guest experience and social-media visibility
- Fast installation compared to conventional cabins
- Flexible deployment for seasonal resort programming

## Considerations

- Privacy management requires careful siting, orientation, and screening
- Solar gain and thermal comfort need active climate-control strategy
- Membrane durability and cleaning protocols affect long-term performance
