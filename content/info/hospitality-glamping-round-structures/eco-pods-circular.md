# Eco-Pods (Circular)

Circular eco-pods are small round accommodation units built for eco-resorts, retreat sites, and nature lodges. Their compact geometry and modular delivery make them effective for phased hospitality expansion.

## Defining Features

- Small-footprint circular cabins
- Efficient envelope-to-floor-area ratio
- Modular or prefabricated construction pathways
- Designed for low-impact site disturbance

## Typical Program

- Sleeping and lounge zone in one integrated room
- Compact wet core for bathroom and services
- Small deck or threshold space for outdoor experience

## Why Operators Use Them

- Scalable rollout across multiple pads or clusters
- Predictable maintenance and replacement cycles
- Strong alignment with eco-tourism brand positioning

## Considerations

- Utility servicing strategy is critical in remote sites
- Moisture and ventilation detailing must be robust in humid climates
- Accessibility and circulation should be planned at site level, not per pod only
