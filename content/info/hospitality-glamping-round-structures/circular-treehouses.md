# Circular Treehouses

Circular treehouses place round hospitality units above ground, either suspended from trees or supported by independent stilts near canopy height. They are popular for premium eco-lodging and destination resorts.

## Defining Features

- Round platform or enclosure geometry
- Elevated position for views, privacy, and experiential value
- Lightweight structural systems adapted to sensitive sites
- Strong connection to forest or woodland setting

## Structural Approaches

- Tree-attached systems with movement-tolerant connectors
- Independent stilt or pile systems to reduce tree stress
- Hybrid systems balancing ecological impact and stability

## Design Considerations

- Arborist and structural-engineer coordination is essential
- Dynamic movement and vibration control affect comfort
- Access, egress, and emergency planning must be addressed early
- Moisture, rot resistance, and long-term maintenance are critical
