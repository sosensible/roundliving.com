# Hospitality & Glamping Round Structures

Round forms are increasingly popular in hospitality because they create immersive guest experiences, strong visual identity, and efficient compact layouts. This category focuses on accommodations designed for resorts, eco-camps, and destination stays.

## Building Types

- [Bubble Domes](/info/hospitality-glamping-round-structures/bubble-domes)
- [Eco-Pods (Circular)](/info/hospitality-glamping-round-structures/eco-pods-circular)
- [Canvas Domes](/info/hospitality-glamping-round-structures/canvas-domes)
- [Circular Treehouses](/info/hospitality-glamping-round-structures/circular-treehouses)

## Quick Comparison

- **Bubble domes** prioritize transparency and immersive views.
- **Circular eco-pods** emphasize compact low-impact hospitality units.
- **Canvas domes** blend luxury glamping comfort with panoramic glazing.
- **Circular treehouses** elevate round living into canopy-level experiences.
