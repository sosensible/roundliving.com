# Canvas Domes

Canvas domes are engineered glamping units built from tensioned fabric systems over circular frames. They bridge tent-like ambience with cabin-level comfort through insulation packages, hard floors, and curated interior finishes.

## Defining Features

- Circular or polygonal dome frame with fabric shell
- Large panoramic windows or transparent front segments
- Elevated hospitality interior standards (beds, heating/cooling, ensuite options)
- Rapid deployment relative to conventional construction

## Guest Experience Strengths

- High visual immersion with weather protection
- Distinctive luxury-with-nature positioning
- Flexible amenity levels from minimalist to high-end suites

## Considerations

- Fabric lifespan and replacement cycles affect lifecycle cost
- Wind/snow loading requirements vary by climate and must be engineered
- Acoustic privacy may require additional interior treatment
