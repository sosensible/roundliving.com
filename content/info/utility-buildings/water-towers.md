# Water Towers

Water towers use elevated circular tanks and support structures to maintain pressure in distribution systems. Their geometry and engineering priorities make them strong examples of functional round design.

## Defining Features

- Elevated tank volume on a column or multi-leg support
- Circular tank sections for pressure and material efficiency
- Access ladders, platforms, and service interfaces

## Design Logic

- Height provides gravitational pressure without constant pumping
- Circular tanks distribute hoop stresses effectively
- Durable materials support long infrastructure lifecycles

## Reuse Potential

- Observation spaces and small public amenities
- Niche hospitality and landmark conversions
- Utility heritage interpretation projects
