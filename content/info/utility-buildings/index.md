# Utility Buildings

Utility-first circular structures were built for work, storage, and resilience. Today, many are also studied or converted for adaptive reuse into studios, event spaces, and homes.

## Building Types

- [Windmills](/info/utility-buildings/windmills)
- [Round Barns](/info/utility-buildings/round-barns)
- [Water Towers](/info/utility-buildings/water-towers)
- [Grain Bins & Silos](/info/utility-buildings/grain-bins-silos)

## Why Utility Typologies Matter

- They demonstrate round geometry under real operational demands
- They offer lessons in efficient material use and weather performance
- They create strong opportunities for adaptive reuse projects

## Quick Comparison

- **Windmills** center on mechanical function and tower-driven vertical structure
- **Round barns** optimize farm circulation and enclosed volume
- **Water towers** prioritize elevated storage and structural durability
- **Grain bins and silos** provide economical cylindrical shells for conversion
