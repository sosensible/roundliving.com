# Round Barns

Round barns emerged in multiple agricultural regions as a practical response to material efficiency and farm workflow. Their circular form enables smooth circulation and robust enclosure with relatively compact structural systems.

## Defining Features

- Circular walls and often conical or domed roofs
- Open interior volume with radial framing logic
- Efficient movement paths for storage and livestock operations

## Why They Were Built

- Lower wall area relative to enclosed volume
- Improved wind performance over rectangular barns in exposed sites
- Simplified circulation around central work zones

## Modern Uses

- Event venues and agritourism sites
- Studios, workshops, and maker barns
- Residential or mixed-use conversions where codes allow
