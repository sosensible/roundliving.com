# Windmills

Windmills are among the most recognizable circular utility structures, combining tower geometry with rotating mechanical systems. Historic examples powered milling and pumping; contemporary versions also inform micro-energy and reuse projects.

## Defining Features

- Vertical tower with circular or polygonal plan
- Rotating sails or turbine components
- Elevated mechanical core and service access
- Strong wind-oriented structural detailing

## Architectural Lessons

- Tall circular forms shed wind efficiently
- Compact footprints can support substantial vertical function
- Repetition and modularity simplify maintenance

## Adaptive Reuse Ideas

- Small guest units or lookout cabins
- Workshop or maker space towers
- Heritage tourism and educational exhibits
