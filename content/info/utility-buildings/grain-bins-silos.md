# Grain Bins & Silos

Grain bins and silos are ubiquitous cylindrical utility structures optimized for bulk storage, weather resilience, and straightforward construction. Their shells are now frequently studied for conversion into small homes, offices, and hospitality units.

## Defining Features

- Cylindrical steel or concrete shell geometry
- Vertical storage logic with gravity-assisted handling
- Standardized dimensions and repeatable fabrication

## Why They Translate to Reuse

- Strong prefabricated shell systems
- Distinctive identity with compact footprint
- Potentially lower shell acquisition cost in rural contexts

## Conversion Considerations

- Insulation and condensation control are critical
- Openings, egress, and floor insertion need engineered detailing
- Utility routing and code compliance vary by jurisdiction
