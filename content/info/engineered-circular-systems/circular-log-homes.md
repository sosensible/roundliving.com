# Circular Log Homes

Circular log homes adapt traditional log-building methods to a radial layout, producing robust walls and a distinctive handcrafted interior feel.

## Defining Features

- Log wall systems arranged on circular footprints
- Heavy-mass envelope with strong visual texture
- High durability when properly detailed and maintained
- Rustic-to-refined design range depending on finish strategy

## Structural and Build Notes

- Corner and connection detailing differs from rectangular log homes
- Differential settling and shrinkage require engineered allowances
- Roof geometry and load paths must align with radial wall behavior

## Why People Choose This Type

- Strong natural-material identity
- Long service life with proper maintenance
- Good thermal inertia in seasonal climates

## Considerations

- Skilled labor is essential for tight joints and long-term performance
- Maintenance cycles (sealing, checking, weathering) should be planned
- Curved layout can constrain standard furniture placement
