# Silo Homes

Silo homes repurpose cylindrical grain or feed silos as housing shells. They are a strong example of adaptive reuse, combining circular geometry with existing industrial structures.

## Defining Features

- Reuse of existing steel or concrete cylindrical shells
- Compact floor plates arranged vertically
- Distinctive industrial-modern character
- Reduced material demand through reuse

## Typical Conversion Priorities

- Structural assessment of shell condition and foundation
- New floor inserts and interior framing strategy
- Insulation, condensation control, and thermal bridging mitigation
- Openings for doors, windows, and egress compliance

## Why People Choose Silo Homes

- Strong design identity with relatively small footprints
- Opportunity for adaptive reuse and lower embodied carbon
- Good fit for studio, guesthouse, or compact primary dwelling use

## Considerations

- Existing shell condition varies widely and can drive cost
- Curved interior walls require custom cabinetry and detailing
- Utility routing and stair design require early coordination
