# Steel-Framed Circular Homes

Steel-framed circular homes are built for precision and long spans. Their structural efficiency supports clean, modern interiors with fewer interruptions and flexible program layouts.

## Defining Features

- Precision-fabricated steel frames for accurate circular geometry
- Large-span capability with minimal interior supports
- Compatibility with modern, minimalist envelopes and detailing
- Strong performance in complex multi-story or exposed-site designs

## Typical Use Cases

- Contemporary custom residences
- High-wind or coastal projects needing robust framing systems
- Designs prioritizing open plans and broad glazing

## Design Considerations

- Thermal bridging requires careful envelope detailing
- Corrosion protection strategy depends on site exposure
- Fabrication lead time and connection coordination affect schedule
- Fire protection requirements may influence finish assemblies
