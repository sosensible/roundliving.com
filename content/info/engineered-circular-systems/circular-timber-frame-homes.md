# Circular Timber Frame Homes

Circular timber frame homes apply post-and-beam logic to radial geometry. Primary posts and ring beams establish a structural rhythm that supports open interiors and expressive craft detailing.

## Defining Features

- Post-and-beam structure arranged around a circular plan
- Radial roof framing converging to a central ring or compression point
- Exposed timber as both structure and finish expression
- Highly customizable interior partitions

## Why They Are Popular

- Strong architectural character with warm natural material
- Long spans and open plans without dense interior bearing walls
- Well suited to premium custom homes and retreat projects

## Design Considerations

- Joinery precision and fabrication quality are critical
- Moisture and movement detailing must be handled carefully
- Curved exterior envelope and glazing packages can increase cost
