# Round Buildings

Round buildings appear in many forms, from high-performance engineered domes to portable traditional dwellings. This guide introduces the main groupings in this site and links to each category hub.

## Circular & Roundhouse Structures

Circular and roundhouse structures bridge historic typologies and contemporary interpretations through diverse material systems and construction techniques.

- Main page: [Circular & Roundhouse Structures](/info/circular-roundhouse-structures)
- Includes: traditional roundhouses, modern roundhouses, cob roundhouses, adobe roundhouses, stone roundhouses

## Curved & Organic Architecture

Curved and organic typologies explore sculptural geometry, fluid interior flow, and nature-inspired form beyond strict circular plans.

- Main page: [Curved & Organic Architecture](/info/curved-organic-architecture)
- Includes: elliptical homes, curvilinear concrete homes, shell structures, circular courtyard homes

## Domes

Domes are among the most structurally efficient building forms, using curvature to distribute loads evenly.

- Main page: [Domes](/info/domes)
- Includes: geodesic domes, monolithic domes

## Earth Integrated

Earth-integrated round homes use soil as a structural, thermal, or landscape element to improve efficiency, resilience, and site harmony.

- Main page: [Earth Integrated](/info/earth-integrated)
- Includes: earthbag roundhouses, earth-sheltered domes, hobbit-style round homes

## Engineered Circular Systems

Engineered systems focus on precision, repeatable geometry, and predictable structural performance across both custom and prefabricated round-home approaches.

- Main page: [Engineered Circular Systems](/info/engineered-circular-systems)
- Includes: silo homes, circular timber frame homes, circular log homes, steel-framed circular homes, panelized round homes

## Experimental & Futuristic Round Homes

Experimental and futuristic round-home systems explore rapid deployment, adaptive mechanics, and emerging fabrication technologies.

- Main page: [Experimental & Futuristic Round Homes](/info/experimental-futuristic-round-homes)
- Includes: inflatable dome homes, 3D-printed round homes, rotating circular homes, spherical homes, pod-based circular homes

## Hospitality & Glamping Round Structures

Hospitality and glamping round structures are designed for immersive guest experiences, compact deployment, and memorable place-based stays.

- Main page: [Hospitality & Glamping Round Structures](/info/hospitality-glamping-round-structures)
- Includes: bubble domes, circular eco-pods, canvas domes, circular treehouses

## In Nature

Natural round shelters and enclosures show how animals solve structural, thermal, and protective problems with curved form, layered materials, and compact geometry.

- Main page: [In Nature](/info/in-nature)
- Includes: beaver lodges, cocoons and chrysalides, bird nests, termite mounds

## Indigenous & Vernacular Round Homes

Indigenous and vernacular circular homes capture place-based building knowledge, local material use, and climate-adapted design evolved over generations.

- Main page: [Indigenous & Vernacular Round Homes](/info/indigenous-vernacular-round-homes)
- Includes: African rondavels, Celtic roundhouses, Native American roundhouses, Pacific island round huts, stump houses

## Influencers

The round-building movement is also shaped by people whose design ideas and teaching changed how circular structures are built and understood.

- Main page: [Influencers](/info/people)
- Includes: Buckminster Fuller, Bill Coperthwaite

## Towers & Lighthouses

Tower and lighthouse-inspired homes focus on vertical living, strong cylindrical form, and elevated views in compact footprints.

- Main page: [Towers & Lighthouses](/info/towers-lighthouses)
- Includes: lighthouse homes, tower houses, observation tower homes

## Utility Buildings

Utility-oriented circular structures show how round geometry performs under practical operational demands such as storage, mechanics, and weather exposure.

- Main page: [Utility Buildings](/info/utility-buildings)
- Includes: windmills, round barns, water towers, grain bins and silos

## Yurts

Yurts combine circular living with practical shelter design, from traditional Mongolian gers to modern engineered and hard-shell systems.

- Main page: [Yurts](/info/yurts)
- Includes: traditional Mongolian yurts (gers), modern engineered yurts, hard-shell yurts
