# African Rondavels

Rondavels are traditional circular dwellings common in several regions of southern Africa. Their compact footprint, thick walls, and steep conical roofs create durable, climate-responsive homes with a strong vernacular identity.

## Defining Features

- Circular plans with earthen, masonry, or plastered walls
- Conical thatched roofs with deep overhangs
- Locally sourced materials and labor-efficient methods
- Strong thermal comfort in hot daytime conditions

## Climate Strategy

- Thick wall mass moderates indoor temperature swings
- High roof volumes improve air movement and heat stratification
- Wide eaves protect walls from rain and reduce splash erosion

## Contemporary Relevance

Rondavel principles continue to influence modern eco-lodges and low-impact housing, especially where material locality and passive performance are priorities.
