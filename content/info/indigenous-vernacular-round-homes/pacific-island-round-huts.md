# Pacific Island Round Huts

Pacific island round huts are lightweight circular structures developed for humid, windy, and coastal conditions. They typically rely on bamboo or timber frames and thatched roofs that shed heavy rainfall while encouraging ventilation.

## Defining Features

- Circular or near-circular footprint
- Bamboo or timber primary structure
- Thatched roof assemblies with generous overhang
- Elevated or ventilated floor systems in flood-prone areas

## Climate Performance

- Porous wall and roof assemblies support cross-ventilation
- Lightweight components reduce structural risk in severe storms
- Rapid repair and replacement is possible with local materials

## Contemporary Relevance

These systems offer valuable models for low-carbon tropical architecture, especially where mechanical cooling can be reduced through passive airflow design.
