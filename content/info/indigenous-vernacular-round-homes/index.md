# Indigenous & Vernacular Round Homes

Many of the most durable and climate-smart circular homes come from indigenous and vernacular building traditions. These structures evolved over generations, using local material systems and region-specific techniques rather than industrial standardization.

## Why This Group Matters

- Demonstrates long-term performance from place-based knowledge
- Uses renewable or locally sourced material palettes
- Connects building form with social and ceremonial life
- Offers lessons for low-impact contemporary design
