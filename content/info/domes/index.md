# Domes

Domes are among the most structurally efficient forms in architecture, distributing loads across curved surfaces and minimizing materials for enclosed volume.

## Dome Building Types

- [Geodesic Domes](/info/domes/geodesic-domes)
- [Monolithic Domes](/info/domes/monolithic-domes)

## Quick Comparison

- **Geodesic domes** use triangulated frameworks and can be lightweight or modular.
- **Monolithic domes** are cast-in-place concrete shells optimized for resilience and long-term durability.

Explore each type to compare construction methods, performance, and living experience.
