# Influencers

Round-building culture is shaped as much by people as by structures. This section highlights thinkers, designers, and builders whose ideas changed how we understand shelter, geometry, and living well in circular space.

## Featured Profiles

- [Buckminster Fuller](/info/people/buckminster-fuller)
- [Bill Coperthwaite](/info/people/bill-coperthwaite)

## Why These Profiles Matter

- They developed or advanced practical round-building methods
- They connected building form with social and ecological values
- Their work still influences contemporary homes, community spaces, and educational models
