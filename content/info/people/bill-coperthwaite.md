# Bill Coperthwaite

Bill Coperthwaite (1930-2013) was an American teacher, designer, and social thinker who helped introduce modern yurt building to North America. Through practical workshops, writing, and community-based education, he connected shelter design with values of simplicity, dignity, and local craftsmanship.

## Key Contributions

- Popularized modern yurt construction in North America
- Emphasized hand-built, low-cost, human-scale housing
- Linked architecture with education, cooperation, and community life

## Why He Matters to Round Building

Coperthwaite demonstrated that round dwellings could be both practical and humane. His yurts were not only structures but also teaching tools for resilience, self-reliance, and intentional living.

## Lasting Influence

His influence continues in:

- Contemporary yurt schools and workshops
- Owner-built housing movements
- Communities exploring low-impact, place-based living
